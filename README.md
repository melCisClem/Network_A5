networking assignment 5

Visual Studio 2022 with "Desktop development with C++" installed
ISO C++ 17 Standard
opengl
glfw3.4


### Tank Game controls
- W to move forward
- S to reverse
- A to turn left
- D to turn right
- SPACE to shoot

The red bar above the tank represents your hp
The blue bar represents the shot cooldown that turns green when ready to shoot

A white outline will highlight the you as the player
